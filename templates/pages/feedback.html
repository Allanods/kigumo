Your Voice Matters in Building Our School

Kigumo Bendera High School is committed to upholding the highest standards of safety, fairness, academic excellence, and accountability.

This dedicated portal allows students, parents, guardians, faculty, and community stakeholders to submit constructive suggestions, compliments, or formal grievances directly to the School Grievance Committee and the Office of the Principal.

Non-Retaliation & Confidentiality Guarantee

All reports submitted through this portal are treated with the strictest confidentiality. You have the absolute right to report anonymously. No student or guardian shall experience prejudice for speaking up in good faith.

Resolution Procedure

1
Submission & Reference ID: Your report is received and assigned a unique tracking reference code.
2
Independent Review: The Grievance Desk investigates facts with utmost discretion and fairness.
3
Action & Feedback: Corrective measures are taken, and written feedback is provided within 5 business days for non-anonymous submissions.

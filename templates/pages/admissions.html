Welcome to the Admissions Desk

Admission to Kigumo Bendera High School is conducted on merit, character, and compliance with the Ministry of Education guidelines.

Each academic year, we welcome Form 1 students placed through the National NEMIS/Ministry of Education selection portal. We also consider a limited number of merit-based transfer applications for Form 2 and Form 3, contingent upon available dormitory bed spaces and curriculum continuity.

We take great pride in our welcoming, well-structured orientation program that assists every new scholar in smoothly transitioning into secondary boarding life.

Required Reporting Documents

All joining scholars must present the following mandatory documents during admission day:

  • Original Ministry Calling Letter Downloaded and stamped from the MoE / NEMIS portal.
  • Assessment Result Slip / KCPE Certificate Original and 2 clear photocopies.
  • Birth Certificate & Parent ID Copies Candidate birth certificate and copies of parent/guardian National ID.
  • Completed Medical Examination Form Certified by a registered Government Medical Officer.
  • Recent Passport Photographs Four (4) colored passport size photos of the student.
Financial Information

Fee Structure & Support

As a public institution, tuition is subsidized under the Government Free Day Secondary Education (FDSE) initiative. Boarding fees and development levies strictly adhere to the Ministry of Education fee guidelines.

Official Fee Schedule

Detailed termly fee breakdowns and approved bank account details are specified in the official school joining instructions:

Cash payments are not accepted at the school cash office. All payments must be deposited to authorized bank accounts or via designated school Paybill.
